# Fictional tabletop injects

This packet is a paper exercise. There are no live accounts, devices, domains, or malicious files.

| Exercise time (UTC) | Inject | Facts provided | Discussion prompt |
|---|---|---|---|
| 09:00 | Employee report | A fictional employee reports an unexpected “shared document” message. They have not opened the attachment. | What should the employee preserve and report? Who owns message analysis? |
| 09:10 | Identity alert | Synthetic logs show an unusual sign-in for alias `user-04`; MFA was approved. | What is confirmed? What evidence checks whether the user approved it? |
| 09:20 | Endpoint report | A training workstation shows several sample file names with a `.locked` suffix. No executable sample is supplied. | What actions reduce spread while preserving evidence? Who can authorize isolation? |
| 09:35 | Business impact | A shared training folder is unavailable; a clean backup from the previous day is listed but not yet verified. | What must be validated before recovery? What dependencies need owner input? |
| 09:50 | External question | A fictional customer asks whether their information was affected. No exfiltration evidence is available. | Who coordinates communications and legal/privacy review? What can be stated accurately now? |
| 10:10 | Recovery update | The backup integrity check passes in the exercise and identity operations revoke the synthetic session. | What validation and monitoring are required before declaring recovery? |
